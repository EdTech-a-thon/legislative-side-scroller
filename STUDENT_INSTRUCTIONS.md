# Capitol Hill Crawler: Student Guide

## Your Mission

You are a new U.S. Representative. Your goal is to pass the fictional **Connected Classrooms Act**, a bill that helps public schools improve internet access, learning tools, accessibility, and teacher support.

To complete the game, move the bill through the House, Senate, and White House.

## Getting Started

1. Create your Representative.
2. Choose your state or territory for location-based civics questions.
3. Start in the Rotunda and enter the House.
4. Follow Mr. Ben Venello's introduction, then explore on your own.

## Moving and Talking

- Move with the arrow keys or `W`, `A`, `S`, and `D`.
- Press `E` or `Enter` near an available Representative to talk.
- A white `!` means the person is available.
- A green check means you secured that person's support.
- A red `X` means that person has no more normal attempts.
- A gold `H` means that Senator has a Hold you need to resolve first.

## Civics Questions

- Read every question carefully.
- Multiple-choice questions use related answer choices. Choose the best answer.
- Open-ended questions may accept short, correct answers, common wording, and close misspellings.
- If a question asks for several answers, enter one answer at a time.
- If you miss an NPC's first question, you get one immediate second attempt with a different question.
- Questions do not normally repeat during a campaign, so use the answer and study note after a miss.

## Study Tools

### Notebook

The Notebook has two tabs:

- **Civics Entries:** Question-and-answer study material you discover through the Shop, tourists, message boards, quests, and map finds.
- **Vocabulary Guide:** Key government terms used in the game.

You cannot open the Notebook during a live question, but you can study it between conversations.

### Rotunda Shop

Correct answers earn **Influence (INF)**. Spend it carefully:

- **Notebook Study Entry:** Adds a new question and answer to your Notebook.
- **Cafeteria Coffee:** Pauses a timed question for 15 seconds.
- **Talking Points Memo:** Removes one wrong multiple-choice answer.
- **Hearing Aid:** Helps with a mumbled question.
- **Super PAC Injection:** Buy it before the Filibuster. It can clear five Rogue Senator Nodes during Senate Overtime.

## Passing the Bill

### House

- You need **218 House votes** to pass the bill.
- At 175 votes, the Committee Dungeon opens.
- Four committee approvals pass the markup and earn stronger support.
- You can keep exploring the House after passage to earn more support, INF, and study material.
- **290 House votes** is the House requirement for a possible Veto Override.

### Senate

- You need **60 Senate votes** for Cloture.
- A Filibuster can delay the bill at 50 votes.
- If you fail the Filibuster, use Senate Overtime:
  - Find Rogue Senator Nodes in the Cloakrooms and Subway.
  - Or persuade an Extreme Senate leader after scouting them.

### White House

- Prepare in the White House hallway before entering the Oval Office.
- President DiSantis may be supportive or skeptical.
- Review your Notebook, Bill Scroll, and Shop items first.

## Optional D.C. Exploration

- Explore D.C. for Notebook finds, tourists, study content, and special encounters.
- At night, Wren, Fritz, Naomi, and Dez may appear in more relaxed locations.
- The Washington Monument and Lincoln Memorial have hidden endgame secrets for dedicated explorers.

## Completion and Submission

When the Connected Classrooms Act becomes law:

1. Review your final score.
2. Choose **Lock In Score**.
3. Click **Download Completion Record**.
4. Attach the downloaded PNG to the Google Classroom assignment.

Your Completion Record shows your Representative name, bill-completion result, House and Senate support, Notebook progress, remaining INF, and final score.

**Your grade is based on completing the game and submitting the Completion Record. Score is mainly for friendly competition.**

## Quick Tips

- Read the answer feedback after a miss.
- Use the Vocabulary Guide when government terms are unfamiliar.
- Talk to more than just the first NPC you see.
- Study before challenging difficult leaders.
- Save at the Rotunda.
- Ask for help if a question, map location, or game rule is confusing.
